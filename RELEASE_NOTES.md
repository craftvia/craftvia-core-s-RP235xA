## Assembly population and MCU variants

The BOM contains 23 rows and 37 component designators. The four external-Flash parts remain in the BOM with variant-aware DNP metadata so a fresh export preserves both supported configurations.

Default `RP2354A + internal 2 MB Flash` assembly:

- Fit U1 as `RP2354A`.
- Do not fit `U2`, `R7`, `R8` or `C17` (`DNP=RP2354A`).
- The RP2354A package-internal 2 MB Flash is the primary boot storage.

Alternate `RP2350A + external 16 MB Flash` assembly:

- Substitute U1 with `RP2350A` using the same QFN-60 footprint.
- Fit `U2=W25Q128JVSIQ`, `R7=0 ohm`, `R8=10 kohm` and `C17=100 nF`.
- U2 is the primary boot storage.

Do not mix the RP2354A MCU with the RP2350A external-Flash population in this Rev0 release. RP2354A plus a secondary external Flash requires a separately reviewed CS connection and boot configuration.

Additional population notes:

- `R1` is 0 ohm by default. Replace it with a same-footprint ferrite bead only after power-noise, ADC and EMI evaluation, and only after checking current rating, DC resistance and bias behavior.
- J8 is a product-specific auxiliary GPIO header for GPIO20-GPIO23 and is not part of the Core-S common interface. It appears as fitted in the exported BOM/CPL; the physical pin header may be omitted for a manual build, provided that the assembly record notes the deviation.
- `TP1`-`TP5` are physical test pads/holes, not parts to be purchased or placed. `TP1` is `VTREF (+3V3_CORE)`; TP2 is GND; TP3/TP4 are SWDIO/SWCLK; TP5 is RUN.
- All CPL rows are on the Top side and use valid 0/90/180/270-degree rotations.

For a turnkey assembly order, create and archive a variant-specific BOM and CPL. For the RP2354A variant, remove `U2`, `R7`, `R8`, `C17` and `TP1`-`TP5` from placement. For the RP2350A variant, remove `TP1`-`TP5`, change U1 to RP2350A and retain all four external-Flash parts.

## Electrical and implementation configuration

- Core-S input is 3.3 V through `R1` to `+3V3_CORE`; no onboard input regulator or input-protection stage is provided.
- U1's internal regulator generates `+1V1`; L1 and the associated bulk/decoupling network follow the RP235xA implementation.
- The 12 MHz crystal network is fitted.
- Native USB is exposed as N7=`USB_DM` and N8=`USB_DP`, with 27-ohm series resistors close to U1.
- BOOTSEL is requested through N4 and the 1-kohm series connection to `QSPI_SS`; use the documented low-during-RUN-reset sequence and return N4 to high impedance afterward.
- SWD debug uses N1=RUN, N2=SWDIO and N3=SWCLK.
- J8 exposes GPIO20-GPIO23 on a product-specific 1 x 4, 2.54 mm PTH footprint.
- U1's exposed GND pad uses a 3 x 3 plated thermal-via array. These vias are for thermal/electrical connection, not hand-solder access holes.

## First-article checks

1. Verify the manufacturer preview against the specified four-layer stack-up, 25.40 mm outline and 90-ohm USB differential requirement.
2. Inspect Core-S orientation/keying, all header pin-1 positions, U1 pin 1, U2 pin 1 when fitted and X1 orientation before power is applied.
3. Confirm the selected MCU/Flash population exactly matches one of the two supported variants.
4. Inspect the U1 exposed-pad 3 x 3 via array for solder wicking/void risk and use a suitably segmented stencil aperture for hot-plate reflow.
5. Power from a current-limited 3.3 V source; check for a short on `+3V3_CORE`, then verify the internal-regulator `+1V1` rail and current consumption.
6. Verify SWD attach, halt, flash erase/write/verify, RUN reset and recovery independently for RP2354A and RP2350A assemblies.
7. Verify N4 Low plus RUN-reset USB BOOTSEL operation, then application USB enumeration and reconnect behavior.
8. Validate USB through the complete Core-S and Station-S path; Station-S routing/switching must also satisfy the signal-integrity requirement.
9. Exercise all 26 Core-S GPIO signals, ADC0-ADC3, J8 GPIO20-GPIO23 and TP1-TP5 with a continuity fixture or Station-S.
10. Measure 150 MHz operation, regulator ripple, current, temperature, ADC behavior and HSTX behavior before claiming production-level performance.
11. Keep secure-boot, debug-disable and irreversible OTP settings unprogrammed during Rev0 bring-up.

## Known limitations

- This is an engineering prototype release. USB signal integrity through the complete Core-S/Station-S path, both MCU/Flash variants, hot-plate QFN yield, ADC accuracy, HSTX, power consumption and thermal behavior remain first-article validation items.
- The exported BOM names U1 as RP2354A. RP2350A assembly therefore requires an explicit U1 substitution in the assembly record and any variant-specific BOM.
- The exported CPL is not turnkey-ready because it includes DNP candidates and TP1-TP5. Filter and re-audit it before external assembly.
- J8 is included in the BOM/CPL but may be omitted in a manual build; it is not a Core-S compatibility requirement.
- Surface finish and solder-mask color remain order-time selections and must be recorded when an order is placed.
- Regeneration should use EasyEDA Pro `v3.2.209` where practical; a different build must be recorded if a later repack is required.
- The historical `.eprj2` workflow-link name is intentionally accepted for Rev0. Circuit, schematic, PCB and portable-project titles identify RP235xA.
